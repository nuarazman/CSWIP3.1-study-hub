Know the current exam structure.
In the CSWIP-WI-6-92 17th Edition (June 2024), the Welding Inspector theory is listed as Part 1: 80 MCQs in 2 hours; Part 2: 40 MCQs on WPS and welder certification in 1 hour 30 minutes; plus practical inspection of eight specimens in 1 hour 30 minutes. Pass mark stated: 70% for all parts. Confirm the current arrangement with your approved training provider before sitting the exam.

Practical examination rehearsal

The practical assessment requires physical specimen inspection and application of the code supplied by the test centre. This digital checklist helps rehearse the sequence but cannot reproduce real weld specimens or replace hands-on training.

Before each specimen

  • Confirm specimen ID and the supplied acceptance code/edition.
  • Read the applicable criteria before judging the weld.
  • Check access, lighting and safe handling.
  • Inspect systematically from one end to the other.

Inspect and measure

  • Check profile, dimensions, alignment and visible imperfections.
  • Use the appropriate gauge and record actual values.
  • Mark the location and type of each finding accurately.
  • Do not infer subsurface quality from appearance alone.

Make a decision

  • Identify the exact acceptance clause/table.
  • Check units, category and any cumulative limits.
  • Decide acceptable/non-acceptable only from the supplied criterion.
  • If a criterion is unclear, seek clarification rather than inventing a limit.

Complete the report

  • Record specimen ID, location, measured finding and result.
  • Use correct terminology (e.g. undercut, overlap, porosity, crack).
  • Make the report traceable and legible.
  • Review all eight specimen records before submission.
Official format reference: CSWIP-WI-6-92, 17th Edition (June 2024).
